## About

I'm a **Computer Systems Engineering graduate from UET Peshawar** working at the intersection of **AI/ML, data analytics, and computer systems**. I approach projects end to end: understanding the problem, engineering reproducible workflows, validating results, and building useful applications.

My work includes transformer-based language and speech models, document intelligence and biometrics, large-scale Python/SQL analytics, Power BI reporting, and C-based Linux systems programming. I also co-authored a peer-reviewed study on speech emotion recognition.

**Areas of interest:** Applied AI · Machine Learning Engineering · Data Engineering · Business Intelligence · Linux Systems · Research.
