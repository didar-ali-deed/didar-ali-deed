## About

I am a **Computer Systems Engineering graduate from UET Peshawar** (2021–2025) working across **applied AI/ML, data analytics, and computer systems**. I focus on taking ideas from problem framing and data engineering to reproducible modeling, clear evaluation, and working software.

- **AI & ML:** Transformer-based NLP and speech models (BERT, Wav2Vec2), document/biometric pipelines, and FastAPI/web demos.
- **Data & BI:** Large-scale Python and SQL workflows, statistical forecasting, and multi-page Power BI dashboards.
- **Systems:** C/C++ programming on Linux (`/proc`, POSIX, file systems, ncurses) with attention to correctness and reproducibility.
- **Research:** Co-author of a peer-reviewed paper on speech emotion recognition (*VFAST Transactions on Software Engineering*, 2025).

**Focus areas:** Applied AI · ML Engineering · Data Engineering · Business Intelligence · Linux Systems · Applied Research
